# SENIOR FACILITIES AGREEMENT — EXCERPT

**dated 14 March 2022**

**HALVESTON SERVICES GROUP LIMITED** as the Company
**CINDERMOOR CREDIT PARTNERS** as Agent and Security Agent
the financial institutions listed in Schedule 2 as the Original Lenders

*The Company is a portfolio company of **Ardenmoor Capital Partners LLP** (the
"Investor"). This excerpt reproduces Clause 1 (relevant definitions) and Clause

## Clause 1 — Definitions (extract)

**"Adjusted EBITDA"** means, for any Relevant Period, Consolidated EBITDA for that
period as adjusted in accordance with the definition below.

**"Consolidated EBITDA"** means the consolidated operating profit of the Group
before taxation, before Net Finance Charges, and before depreciation,
amortisation and impairment, **plus** the amount of any Permitted Add-Backs
(without double counting), excluding exceptional or unrealised gains.

**"Permitted Add-Backs"** means, without double counting and to the extent
deducted in arriving at operating profit:

  (i) **run-rate cost savings, synergies and operational improvements** which are
  reasonably identifiable and factually supportable, expected to be realised
  within **18 months** of the relevant initiative and certified by the Company,
  **provided that** the aggregate amount added back under this paragraph (i) in
  any Relevant Period shall **not exceed 20 per cent. of Consolidated EBITDA**
  (calculated before giving effect to this paragraph (i));

  (ii) exceptional, one-off or non-recurring costs, including restructuring and
  integration costs;

  (iii) transaction and advisory costs incurred in connection with a Permitted
  Acquisition;

  (iv) any non-cash charge, including share-based payments and non-cash
  impairment.

*(The paragraph (i) test — "reasonably identifiable and factually supportable …
realised within 18 months", capped at 20 per cent. — is identical to the test in
the Investor's other financings.)*

**"Total Net Debt"** means the aggregate outstanding principal amount of all
Financial Indebtedness of the Group (including the Facility and any drawn
Revolving Facility) **less** Cash and Cash Equivalents, per the most recent
Compliance Certificate.

**"Test Date"** means the last day of each Financial Quarter.

## Clause 22 — Financial Covenant

**22.1  Total Net Leverage.** The Company shall ensure that, in respect of each
Relevant Period ending on a Test Date, the ratio of **Total Net Debt to Adjusted
EBITDA** shall not exceed **5.75 : 1**.

**22.2  Interest Cover.** The Company shall ensure that the ratio of Adjusted
EBITDA to Cash Interest is not less than **2.25 : 1**.

**22.3  Testing.** The covenant is tested quarterly by reference to a Compliance
Certificate delivered within 45 days of each Test Date, signed by a director or
the chief financial officer, certifying the calculations and confirming whether
any Default is continuing. A breach is an Event of Default, subject to the
Investor's equity-cure right under Clause 23 (not reproduced here).

## Note 24 — Correction of covenant EBITDA and prior compliance certificates

During the audit for the year ended 31 December 2024, the run-rate cost synergies
added back by the Group in its quarterly Compliance Certificates were reviewed.
The Group had added back **£3.5m** of run-rate cost synergies attributed to the
integration of the 2023 bolt-on acquisition in arriving at Adjusted EBITDA for
covenant purposes (including at the 30 June 2024 Test Date).

On review, **the entire £3.5m was projected rather than reasonably identifiable
and factually supportable, and had not been — and was not expected within the
required period to be — realised.** It therefore does not qualify as a Permitted
Add-Back under limb (i) of the definition of Consolidated EBITDA, and has been
disallowed in full.

### Restated Adjusted EBITDA — LTM to 30 June 2024

| Line | As certified | Adjustment | **Restated** |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Consolidated EBITDA (before add-backs) | 22.5 | — | **22.5** |
| (i) Run-rate cost synergies | 3.5 | (3.5) | **0.0** |
| **Adjusted EBITDA (£m)** | **26.0** | **(3.5)** | **22.5** |

### Effect on the Financial Covenant

Total Net Debt (£140.0m) is unaffected. Re-testing Clause 22.1 on the restated
Adjusted EBITDA:

| Covenant | As certified | **As restated** | Required | Restated status |
|---|---:|---:|---|---|
| Total Net Leverage (£140.0m ÷ Adjusted EBITDA) | 5.38× | **6.22×** (140.0 ÷ 22.5) | ≤ 5.75× | **✗ BREACH** |

The restated ratio gives rise to an Event of Default under the Agreement at the
30 June 2024 Test Date. The Event of Default was **subsequently waived** by the
Lenders under a waiver letter dated 30 April 2025; the covenant level was left
unchanged.

*Plain-English summary (not part of the accounts): Halveston certified its June
2024 quarter as compliant — leverage 5.38× against a 5.75× limit. Once the £3.5m
of run-rate synergies was disallowed by the audit, the same quarter was 6.22× — a
breach. The debt never changed; the add-back was never real. The identical move —
same sponsor, same run-rate-synergy add-back — reappears on the Thornwick deal
two years later.*

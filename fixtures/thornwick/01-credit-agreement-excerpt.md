## Clause 1 — Definitions (extract)

**"Adjusted EBITDA"** means, in respect of any Relevant Period, Consolidated
EBITDA for that period as adjusted in accordance with the definition of
Consolidated EBITDA below.

**"Applicable Margin"** means 3.25 per cent. per annum over the Reference Rate
(SONIA), subject to adjustment in accordance with Clause 12 (Margin) […].

**"Consolidated EBITDA"** means, in respect of any Relevant Period, the
consolidated operating profit of the Group before taxation:

(a) **before** deducting any Net Finance Charges;

(b) **before** deducting any amount attributable to the amortisation, depreciation
or impairment of assets;

(c) **plus** the amount of any Permitted Add-Backs (as defined below), without
double counting;

(d) **excluding** any exceptional, one-off or non-recurring gain, and any
unrealised gain on any financial instrument or on the revaluation of any asset;

(e) **excluding** the results of any member of the Group acquired or disposed of
during the Relevant Period except to the extent attributable to the period of
ownership,

in each case determined on the basis of the most recent consolidated financial
statements delivered under Clause 20 (Information Undertakings) and calculated on
a Pro Forma Basis for any acquisition or disposal.

**"Permitted Add-Backs"** means, without double counting and to the extent
deducted in arriving at consolidated operating profit:

  (i) **run-rate cost savings, synergies and operational improvements** which are
  reasonably identifiable and factually supportable, expected to be realised
  within **18 months** of the relevant initiative and certified by the Company,
  **provided that** the aggregate amount added back under this paragraph (i) in
  any Relevant Period shall **not exceed 20 per cent. of Consolidated EBITDA**
  (calculated before giving effect to this paragraph (i));

  (ii) exceptional, one-off, non-recurring or extraordinary costs, charges or
  expenses, including restructuring, reorganisation and integration costs;

  (iii) transaction costs, advisory, legal and financing fees incurred in
  connection with the Acquisition or any Permitted Acquisition;

  (iv) any non-cash charge, including any charge in respect of share-based
  payments and any non-cash impairment.

**"Cash Interest"** means, in respect of any Relevant Period, the aggregate
amount of Net Finance Charges paid or payable in cash by members of the Group in
that period.

**"Financial Covenants"** means the covenants set out in Clause 22.

**"LTM"** means, in respect of any Test Date, the period of twelve months ending
on that Test Date.

**"Relevant Period"** means each period of twelve months ending on a Test Date.

**"Test Date"** means the last day of each Financial Quarter (31 March, 30 June,
30 September and 31 December).

**"Total Net Debt"** means, at any time, the aggregate outstanding principal
amount of all Financial Indebtedness of the Group (including the Facilities and
any drawn amount under the Revolving Facility) **less** the aggregate amount of
Cash and Cash Equivalents of the Group at that time, as set out in the most
recent Compliance Certificate.

## Clause 22 — Financial Covenants

**22.1  Total Net Leverage.** The Company shall ensure that, in respect of each
Relevant Period ending on a Test Date, the ratio of **Total Net Debt to Adjusted
EBITDA** shall not exceed the level set out opposite that Test Date:

| Test Date falling | Total Net Debt : Adjusted EBITDA not to exceed |
|---|---|
| on or before 31 December 2026 | **6.50 : 1** |
| 31 March 2027 to 31 December 2027 (inclusive) | 6.25 : 1 |
| on or after 31 March 2028 | 6.00 : 1 |

**22.2  Interest Cover.** The Company shall ensure that, in respect of each
Relevant Period ending on a Test Date, the ratio of **Adjusted EBITDA to Cash
Interest** shall not be less than **2.00 : 1**.

**22.3  Testing.** The Financial Covenants are tested quarterly by reference to
each Compliance Certificate delivered under Clause 20.3, which must be delivered
within 45 days of each Test Date and signed by a director or the chief financial
officer of the Company, certifying the calculations in reasonable detail and
confirming whether any Default is continuing.

**22.4  Breach.** A failure to comply with Clause 22.1 or 22.2 on any Test Date
is an Event of Default, **subject to Clause 23 (Equity Cure)**.

## Clause 23 — Equity Cure

**23.1** If the Company fails to comply with a Financial Covenant on a Test Date,
the Investor may (but is not obliged to) cure that breach by procuring that an
amount of **Cure Amount** is received by the Group by way of subscription for
ordinary shares in, or the provision of Subordinated Debt to, the Company, within
**20 Business Days** after the date on which the relevant Compliance Certificate
is (or should have been) delivered.

**23.2** The Cure Amount is, at the Investor's election, deemed to (a) increase
Adjusted EBITDA for the relevant Relevant Period and each subsequent Relevant
Period in which that period is included, or (b) reduce Total Net Debt, in each
case solely for the purpose of re-testing the Financial Covenants.

**23.3  Limitations.** No more than **five** Cure Amounts may be provided over
the life of the Facilities; no more than **two** in any period of four
consecutive Financial Quarters; and a Cure Amount may not be applied in respect
of **two consecutive** Test Dates.

## Clause 24 — Restricted Payments (extract)

**24.1** No member of the Group shall make a Restricted Payment, except:

(a) out of a general basket not exceeding **£5,000,000** in any financial year;
and

(b) out of the **Available Amount** (an amount equal to 50 per cent. of
Consolidated Net Income accrued from the Closing Date), **provided that** at the
time of, and after giving pro forma effect to, that Restricted Payment (i) no
Default is continuing and (ii) the Total Net Leverage ratio would be **less than
5.50 : 1**.

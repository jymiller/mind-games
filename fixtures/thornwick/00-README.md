# Thornwick Logistics — synthetic private-credit deal (memory demo pack)

> **FICTIONAL — created for demonstration.** Every entity, person, date and
> figure below is invented. Any resemblance to a real company, fund, firm or
> person is coincidental. This pack contains **no confidential, non-public or
> MNPI** information and is safe to ingest into a third-party memory service.

This is **Deal 1 — the hero** — of a two-deal memory-demo pack (start at
`../START-HERE.html`). Its single moment: **a quarter that reads COMPLIANT on the
numbers first certified becomes a BREACH once a restated EBITDA is applied.**
A memory system should catch that flip by remembering what the earlier
certificate said. (Deal 2, `../deal-2-halveston/`, adds the cross-deal beat: the
same sponsor ran the same play on another deal, two years earlier.)

## The covenants (original credit agreement)

| Covenant | Definition | Direction | Level (as originally agreed) | Test |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Total Net Leverage** | Total Net Debt ÷ LTM Adjusted EBITDA | ceiling | **≤ 6.50×** (step-down to 6.25× from Q1-27, 6.00× thereafter) | quarterly |
| **Interest Cover (ICR)** | LTM Adjusted EBITDA ÷ LTM Cash Interest | floor | **≥ 2.00×** | quarterly |

Breach of either is an Event of Default, subject to a sponsor **equity-cure**
right. "Adjusted EBITDA" is Consolidated EBITDA plus negotiated add-backs —
run-rate cost synergies (capped at 20% of unadjusted EBITDA), exceptional /
non-recurring costs, transaction costs, non-cash charges.

## The flip (the demo moment) — the numbers that tie

**Q1 2026 (test date 31 March 2026), as first certified vs after restatement:**

| Line | As certified (Q1 cert) | After FY2025 restatement |
|---|---:|---:|
| Consolidated EBITDA (pre add-back) | £29.5m | £27.8m  *(−£1.7m early revenue reversed)* |
| + Run-rate cost synergies | £3.2m | £0.2m  *(−£3.0m disallowed: cap + not realised)* |
| + Exceptional / restructuring | £1.0m | £1.0m |
| + Transaction / advisory | £0.3m | £0.0m  *(reclassified recurring)* |
| **= LTM Adjusted EBITDA** | **£34.0m** | **£29.0m** |
| Total Net Debt | £220.0m | £220.0m |
| **Total Net Leverage** (≤ 6.50×) | **6.47× ✓ COMPLIANT** | **7.59× ✗ BREACH** |
| LTM Cash Interest | £16.5m | £16.5m |
| **Interest Cover** (≥ 2.00×) | **2.06× ✓ COMPLIANT** | **1.76× ✗ BREACH** |

The £5.0m EBITDA swing is the whole story, and its biggest single driver is the
**£3.0m disallowed synergies add-back** — the classic "add-back trick."

Prior quarter for context — **Q4 2025 (31 December 2025):** Adjusted EBITDA
£34.2m, Net Debt £217.0m → leverage **6.35×** and ICR **2.10×**, both compliant
with visible headroom. It is the "everything looks fine" baseline the restated
Q1 breaks.

## The memory beats this pack is built to exercise

- **The restatement flip** — "Q1 2026 was certified at 6.47× (compliant); on the
  restated EBITDA it is 7.59× — a breach." *Remembering the first certificate is
  what makes the flip visible.*
- **The amendment** — "Total Net Leverage was 6.50×; after Amendment & Waiver
  No. 1 it is 7.25×." *The term changed; memory should hold both.*
- **The auditor change (tripwire)** — FY2024 was audited by Larkfield Downe LLP;
  FY2025 by Marbury Tolland LLP, and the new auditor is what surfaced the
  restatement. *"Auditor changed → re-scrutinise prior add-backs" is a playbook
  a memory system can pre-arm.*
- **The add-back pattern (cross-deal link)** — the disallowed item is run-rate
  synergies from a bolt-on, and **Deal 2 in this pack (Halveston,
  `../deal-2-halveston/`) is a second Ardenmoor deal that ran the same play two
  years earlier** — so memory can say "this sponsor has done this before."

## What this pack deliberately does NOT contain

No real company, fund or person; no real filing, prospectus or account; no
Companies House number. And nothing from the monitoring platform this was built
alongside — no analytics-engine logic, no grading formula, no proprietary data
model. It is a set of ordinary, invented finance documents. The "memory" is the
demo's job; these are just the inputs.

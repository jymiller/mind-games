## Note 27 — Prior-period restatement and correction of covenant EBITDA

During the audit for the year ended 31 December 2025, two matters were identified
that require restatement of amounts previously reported to the Group's lenders in
the quarterly Compliance Certificates for the quarters ended 31 December 2025 and
31 March 2026. The Directors, with the concurrence of the auditor, have restated
the affected figures.

**(a) Revenue recognition (IFRS 15).** Revenue of **£1.7m** recognised in the
second half of 2025 on two multi-year distribution contracts was recognised
before the related performance obligations had been satisfied. This revenue has
been reversed, reducing Consolidated EBITDA for each Relevant Period in which the
second half of 2025 falls.

**(b) Run-rate synergy add-backs.** The Group added back run-rate cost synergies
of £2.9m (quarter ended 31 December 2025) and £3.2m (quarter ended 31 March 2026)
in arriving at Adjusted EBITDA for covenant purposes. On review, **£3.0m** of the
synergies claimed at 31 March 2026 (and £2.7m of those claimed at 31 December
2025) were **projected rather than reasonably identifiable and factually
supportable, and had not been, and were not expected within the required period
to be, realised**. They do not qualify as Permitted Add-Backs under limb (i) of
the definition of Consolidated EBITDA and have been disallowed; £0.2m in each
quarter is supportable and retained. In addition, £0.3m previously treated as
non-recurring transaction cost has been reclassified as a recurring operating
cost and is no longer added back.

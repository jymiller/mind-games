# AMENDMENT AND WAIVER No. 1

**dated 28 August 2026**

to the Senior Facilities Agreement dated 12 September 2024 (the "**Original
Agreement**")

**between:**
**THORNWICK LOGISTICS HOLDINGS LIMITED** (the "Company")
**FENWATER CREDIT PARTNERS** as Agent (on behalf of the Lenders under the Original
Agreement)

Capitalised terms used but not defined in this deed have the meaning given in the
Original Agreement.

## Recitals

(A) On 3 July 2026 the Company delivered to the Agent its audited consolidated
financial statements for the year ended 31 December 2025, which **restated**
Adjusted EBITDA for the Relevant Periods ending 31 December 2025 and 31 March 2026
(the "**Restatement**"), as more fully described in Note 27 to those statements.

(B) Giving effect to the Restatement, the Company was **not** in compliance with
the Total Net Leverage covenant (Clause 22.1) or the Interest Cover covenant
(Clause 22.2) at the Test Dates falling on 31 December 2025 and 31 March 2026 (the
"**Specified Breaches**"), each of which is an Event of Default under Clause 22.4.

(C) The Company has requested that the Lenders waive the Specified Breaches and
amend the levels of the Financial Covenants, and the Lenders have agreed to do so
on the terms of this deed.

## 1. Waiver

1.1 The Lenders **irrevocably waive** the Specified Breaches and any Event of
Default or Default arising solely from them. This waiver is limited to the
Specified Breaches and is not a waiver of any other or future Default.

## 2. Amendments to the Financial Covenants

2.1 With effect from the Amendment Effective Date, **Clause 22.1 (Total Net
Leverage)** is amended so that the ratio of Total Net Debt to Adjusted EBITDA
shall not exceed:

| Test Date falling | Level under the Original Agreement | **Amended level** |
|---|---|---|
| 30 September 2026 to 31 December 2026 | 6.50 : 1 | **7.25 : 1** |
| 31 March 2027 to 30 September 2027 | 6.25 : 1 | **7.25 : 1** |
| 31 December 2027 | 6.25 : 1 | **6.75 : 1** |
| on or after 31 March 2028 | 6.00 : 1 | **6.25 : 1** |

2.2 With effect from the Amendment Effective Date, **Clause 22.2 (Interest
Cover)** is amended so that, for each Test Date falling on or before 30 September
2027, the ratio of Adjusted EBITDA to Cash Interest shall not be less than
**1.75 : 1** (previously 2.00 : 1). From 31 December 2027 the required ratio
reverts to 2.00 : 1.

*For the avoidance of doubt: the Total Net Leverage covenant level, which was
**6.50×** under the Original Agreement, is **7.25×** under this deed for Test Dates
from 30 September 2026.*

## 3. Consideration and additional terms

3.1 **Margin.** With effect from the Amendment Effective Date the Applicable
Margin is increased by 0.50 per cent. per annum, to **SONIA + 3.75 per cent.**

3.2 **Amendment fee.** The Company shall pay to the Agent, for the account of the
Lenders, a fee of **0.50 per cent.** of the aggregate Commitments, payable on the
Amendment Effective Date.

3.3 **Enhanced reporting.** Until the Test Date falling on 31 December 2027, the
Company shall deliver **monthly** management accounts (in addition to the
quarterly Compliance Certificates), and each Compliance Certificate shall itemise
every Permitted Add-Back with supporting evidence of realisation.

3.4 **Equity cure unaffected.** Clause 23 (Equity Cure) continues in full force;
no Cure Amount was applied in respect of the Specified Breaches.

## 4. Effect of this deed

4.1 The Original Agreement and this deed are to be read as one. Except as amended
by this deed, the Original Agreement remains in full force and effect.

4.2 The "**Amendment Effective Date**" is the date on which the Agent confirms
receipt of this deed executed by each party and payment of the fee in Clause 3.2.

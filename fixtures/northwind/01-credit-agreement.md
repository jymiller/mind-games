# SENIOR SECURED TERM LOAN CREDIT AGREEMENT (Excerpt)

**$300,000,000 Senior Secured Term Loan B**

Dated as of **March 15, 2025**, among **NORTHWIND AGGREGATES, INC.**, a Delaware corporation (the
"Borrower"), the Lenders party hereto, and **BLACKFORD CREDIT PARTNERS LLC**, as Administrative Agent
and Collateral Agent. The Borrower is a portfolio company of **Meridian Equity Partners** (the
"Sponsor"). The Borrower's independent registered public accounting firm as of the Closing Date is
**Halloran LLP**.

---

## ARTICLE I — SELECTED DEFINITIONS

**"Consolidated EBITDA"** means, for any period, Consolidated Net Income for such period, plus,
without duplication and to the extent deducted in computing Consolidated Net Income: (a) consolidated
interest expense; (b) provision for income taxes; (c) depreciation and amortization; (d) non-recurring
or unusual charges not to exceed 10% of Consolidated EBITDA; and (e) the amount of **pro forma "run
rate" cost savings, operating expense reductions and synergies** projected in good faith to result
from actions taken or expected to be taken within **24 months**, net of amounts actually realized;
**provided** that the aggregate amount added back under clause (e) shall not exceed **25% of
Consolidated EBITDA** (calculated after giving effect to such add-backs) for any period (the "Add-Back
Cap").

**"Consolidated Net Debt"** means, as of any date, the aggregate principal amount of Consolidated Total
Funded Debt outstanding, minus unrestricted cash and cash equivalents of the Borrower and its
Restricted Subsidiaries, such netting not to exceed **$25,000,000**.

**"Total Net Leverage Ratio"** means, as of any Test Date, the ratio of Consolidated Net Debt to
Consolidated EBITDA for the most recently ended period of four consecutive fiscal quarters (LTM).

**"Interest Coverage Ratio"** means the ratio of Consolidated EBITDA to Consolidated Cash Interest
Expense, in each case for the LTM period ending on the Test Date.

**"Compliance Certificate"** means a certificate substantially in the form of Exhibit C, executed by a
Financial Officer of the Borrower.

**"Financial Officer"** means the chief financial officer, treasurer, or controller of the Borrower.

## ARTICLE VI — FINANCIAL COVENANTS

**Section 6.01 — Maximum Total Net Leverage Ratio.** The Borrower shall not permit the Total Net
Leverage Ratio as of the last day of any fiscal quarter to exceed the level set forth below for the
period in which such fiscal quarter ends:

| Test period | Maximum Total Net Leverage Ratio |
| --- | --- |
| Closing Date through December 31, 2025 | **6.50x** |
| January 1, 2026 through December 31, 2026 | 6.00x |
| January 1, 2027 and thereafter | 5.50x |

**Section 6.02 — Minimum Interest Coverage Ratio.** The Borrower shall not permit the Interest Coverage
Ratio as of the last day of any fiscal quarter to be less than **2.00x**.

**Section 6.03 — Equity Cure Right.** In the event of a failure to comply with Section 6.01 or 6.02, the
Sponsor may make a cash equity contribution (a "Cure Amount"), which shall be deemed to increase
Consolidated EBITDA for the relevant period. The Equity Cure Right may be exercised **no more than two
(2) times in any fiscal year and no more than five (5) times over the life of the facility**, and no
more than in two consecutive fiscal quarters.

## ARTICLE V — REPORTING (Excerpt)

**Section 5.01(c) — Compliance Certificate.** Within **45 days** after the end of each fiscal quarter,
the Borrower shall deliver a Compliance Certificate executed by a Financial Officer, setting forth
(i) the calculation of the Total Net Leverage Ratio and Interest Coverage Ratio, (ii) a reasonably
detailed reconciliation of Consolidated EBITDA, including each add-back under clause (e) of the
definition thereof, and (iii) whether an Event of Default has occurred.

**Section 5.01(a) — Annual Audited Financials.** Within 90 days after fiscal year-end, audited
consolidated financial statements reported on by the Borrower's independent registered public
accounting firm (**Halloran LLP** as of the Closing Date).

# FIRST AMENDMENT TO CREDIT AGREEMENT (Excerpt)

**FIRST AMENDMENT**, dated as of **September 30, 2025** (this "Amendment"), to the Senior Secured Term
Loan Credit Agreement dated as of March 15, 2025 (the "Credit Agreement"), among **NORTHWIND
AGGREGATES, INC.** (the "Borrower"), the Lenders party hereto, and **BLACKFORD CREDIT PARTNERS LLC**,
as Administrative Agent.

## RECITALS

WHEREAS, the Borrower has requested a $25,000,000 incremental term loan to fund a bolt-on acquisition;
and

WHEREAS, in connection therewith the Lenders have required certain amendments to the definition of
Consolidated EBITDA to strengthen the substantiation of projected add-backs;

NOW, THEREFORE, the parties agree as follows:

## SECTION 1 — INCREMENTAL TERM LOAN

The Lenders party hereto agree to make an incremental term loan to the Borrower in an aggregate
principal amount of **$25,000,000** (the "Incremental Term Loan"), which shall constitute Term Loans
under, and have the same terms as, the existing Term Loans under the Credit Agreement.

## SECTION 2 — AMENDMENT TO "CONSOLIDATED EBITDA"

Clause (e) of the definition of "Consolidated EBITDA" in Article I of the Credit Agreement is amended
and restated to read as follows:

> "(e) the amount of pro forma 'run rate' cost savings, operating expense reductions and synergies
> projected in good faith to result from actions taken or expected to be taken within 24 months, net of
> amounts actually realized; **provided** that (i) the aggregate amount added back under this clause (e)
> shall not exceed **15% of Consolidated EBITDA** for any period (the 'Revised Add-Back Cap'), and
> (ii) any individual add-back in excess of **$1,000,000** shall be **certified by the Borrower's
> independent registered public accounting firm** as satisfying the run-rate substantiation
> requirements set forth on Schedule 1.02, failing which such amount shall be **disallowed**."

## SECTION 3 — CONDITIONS AND RATIFICATION

This Amendment shall become effective upon execution by the Borrower and the Required Lenders. Except
as expressly amended hereby, the Credit Agreement remains in full force and effect and is ratified
and confirmed. The revised Add-Back Cap and auditor-attestation requirement apply to Consolidated
EBITDA determined **for all periods** presented in Compliance Certificates delivered on or after the
effective date of this Amendment.

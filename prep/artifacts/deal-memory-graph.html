Two doors into memory — why one type is missing from the map

This is the piece that's easy to miss, and it answers your question directly: memory doesn't come back one way. Three types answer to search; the procedural type answers only to the trigger tripwire. That's exactly why procedures never appeared in the entity map or the export — they live behind a different door.

So — are we using the types right?

Facts & episodes: yes, doing exactly their job. Artifacts: underused — ingest the cert and the credit agreement as artifacts (versioned documents) so the demo shows document lineage, not just loose facts. Procedures: working (exp4 proved it) — just remember they're a separate channel, so never expect them in the corpus view.

What the shape tells you

Facts are the hubs

Everything points at facts — episodes group them, artifacts are built from them, and facts supersede each other. If you're wiring the demo or an audit trail, the fact and its supersedes edge are the load-bearing joints.

The graph is directional and shallow

Two-way links are rare; most edges flow one way (deal → conversation → turn → memory). The one loop that matters — Fact superseding Fact — is the belief-revision spine, and it's the only edge that reaches backward in time.

Testing complete — what carries into the build

All seven run. Every demo beat is proven on the live API — nothing critical is unknown. What carries into Saturday:

  1. Three phrasing recipes are load-bearing. Supersession fires on a same-slot restatement; the tripwire fires on a directive that names the action's exact identifiers; recall + /revisions together give the lineage answer. Bake these into the seed script.
  2. Pre-warm everything. ~18s extraction is too slow for the stage — ingest all demo data ahead of time so Saturday reads cached memory.
  3. One limitation to design around. Typed retracted won't auto-fire — handle a waiver in Enid's app state, don't lean on it as a beat.
